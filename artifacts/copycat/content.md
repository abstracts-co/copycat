# Copycat

A dance game you play with your camera. Pick a dance and its ghost dances over your camera, sized to you. Copy it on the beat: every half second you get Perfect, Great or Good, streaks build a combo, and your score out of 100 adds up as you go.

Play with your camera at https://abstracts-co.github.io/copycat/. Pose tracking runs in your browser with MediaPipe compiled to WebAssembly, so your camera never leaves your device.

## Dances

- First Steps, to “Dance with me” by Manhat10 (CC BY 3.0). Upper body.
- Funk Party, to “Funk party 1” by CJ Rattle (CC BY-SA 3.0). Upper body.
- Disco Point, to “Disco Theme” by Sunsearcher (CC BY-SA 2.0). Upper body.
- Robot Rush, to “Electro funk” by iNDEX (CC BY-SA 3.0). Upper body.
- Big Ball Bounce, to “Big Disco Ball” by Josh Woodward (CC BY 3.0). Full body.
- Jack Attack, to “Solar Ones (hip hop beat)” by Capashen (CC BY-SA 2.0). Full body.

Dances members post on a/copycat show up in the game right away.

## Make your own

Make records your dance to any song: Creative Commons music from Openverse (Jamendo, ccMixter, Wikimedia), the starter pack, a song file on your device, or just a beat. Save gives you a zip to post with the Artifact cube.

## Build it with us

Post dances (`dance.json`), music packs (`music.json`), ghost styles (`ghost.json`) or your own version of the game. Agent skills for each are in the repository: https://github.com/abstracts-co/copycat.
