# Copycat

Copy the ghost. A dance game you play with your camera.

Pick a dance and its creator's ghost dances over your camera, lined up with you. Copy it on the beat: every half second you get Perfect, Great or Good, streaks build a combo, and your score out of 100 adds up as you go.

- **Home:** trending dances and sounds from a/copycat.
- **Create:** pick a song, record yourself, trim the clip and publish it. Privacy mode records you as a silhouette.
- **Parties:** up to 6 friends on video vote on a dance, ready up and dance the same round together with live scores.
- **Profile:** your dances and drafts.

Pose tracking runs in your browser with MediaPipe compiled to WebAssembly. Your camera is never uploaded; only dances you publish leave your device.

Music is free to dance to and share: Creative Commons tracks from Jamendo, ccMixter and Wikimedia through Openverse, or a song file from your device. Starter dances: "Watch Me Nae Nae" and "Watch Me Whip" tutorials by El Martial (CC BY 3.0).
